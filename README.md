Retention isn't about flashy transitions or $10,000 cameras. It’s about psychology. If you want tech viewers to stick around past the 30-second mark, you need to master the **Open Loop Hook**.

Borrowed from Hollywood screenwriters, an open loop creates an instant curiosity gap. You drop an intriguing question, an unexpected result, or a dramatic claim in the first five seconds—but you *withhold* the payoff until later in the video.

The human brain hates unfinished information. Once you open a loop, the viewer's brain craves closure. They won't click away because they need to see how the story ends.

Here is what this looks like in practice:

**The Standard Hook (Boring):**  
*"Today we’re reviewing the new M3 MacBook Pro and testing its render speeds against last year's model."*  
*(Result: Viewers instantly skip ahead or drop off.)*

**The Open Loop Hook (Addictive):**  
*"This $1,500 laptop just out-rendered my $6,000 desktop studio. But there's one thermal flaw that almost made me send it back..."*  
*(Result: Loop opened. Now they have to watch to find out what the flaw is and how it beat the desktop.)*

To execute this on your channel:

1. **Plant the Seed:** Drop a high-stakes teaser immediately. 
2. **Delay the Payoff:** Build up the context, specs, or tutorial steps first.
3. **Close the Loop:** Deliver the big reveal near the climax of your video.

You can even stack multiple loops throughout a video to keep retention flatlined across a 10-minute script.

Stop giving away the ending in your intro. Open the loop, stretch the tension, and watch your average view duration skyrocket.
