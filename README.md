Retention dropping off at the 30-second mark? You’re not alone. Most tech creators dump specs on their audience before giving them a reason to care. 

Enter the **AIDA model**—a battle-tested copywriting framework that turns passive scrollers into hyper-engaged subscribers. Here is how to map it directly to your next tech review or tutorial:

### 1. Attention (The Hook)
Skip the 10-second channel intro. Grab viewers in the first 3 seconds with a bold claim, a provocative question, or an insane visual.
* **Tech Example:** *"Do NOT buy the new M3 MacBook until you see this thermal test."*

### 2. Interest (The Setup)
Now that you have their eyes, hold them by tapping into a relatable pain point or curiosity gap. Make them realize they *need* this information.
* **Tech Example:** *"On paper, the specs look incredible. But in real-world 4K video editing, there’s a hidden bottleneck nobody is talking about."*

### 3. Desire (The Proof)
This is where you showcase the solution. Deliver the high-value insights, dynamic B-roll, and benchmarks. Make them crave the workflow, gadget, or fix you’re demonstrating.
* **Tech Example:** *"Here is the side-by-side render comparison. By tweaking just two settings, we shaved 4 minutes off export times without spending a dime."*

### 4. Action (The Conversion)
Don't let the video fade to black without a clear directive. Give viewers one friction-free next step while retention is high.
* **Tech Example:** *"If this saved you time, hit subscribe and check the pinned comment for my custom export presets."*

Structure beats algorithm luck every single time. Master the AIDA flow, trim the fluff, and watch your average view duration climb.
